CONFLUENCE RULE USED BY ALL CHAMPIONS

For every candle, calculate bullish and bearish scores from the weighted signals.

Long setup:
  bull_score >= entry_threshold
  bull_score - bear_score >= min_dominance

Short setup:
  bear_score >= entry_threshold
  bear_score - bull_score >= min_dominance

Risk/reward:
  Target uses RR.
  Stop uses swing/structure plus ATR buffer.
